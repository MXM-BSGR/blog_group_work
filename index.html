FAQ

What’s the story behind my travel blog?

It all started with a simple backpack and a one-way ticket, driven by a deep curiosity to see the world beyond the usual tourist paths. What began as a personal journal to keep friends and family updated quickly turned into a passion project dedicated to sharing authentic travel stories, hidden gems, and honest guides for fellow explorers.

How do I plan, document, and share my travels?

I usually start with a rough itinerary and a lot of research, but I always leave room for spontaneous detours. On the road, I capture moments through my camera, daily journal entries, and smartphone notes, which I later turn into structured guides, stories, and tips here on the blog to help you plan your own adventures.

How can brands, companies, or fellow creators collaborate with me or get in touch?

I’m always open to partnering with brands, tourism boards, and creators whose values align with sustainable and meaningful travel. Whether you're interested in sponsored content, photography, or collaborations, you can reach out via the contact form on my site or drop me a direct email.

How can readers support this blog and the work behind it?

The best way to support this blog is simply by reading, sharing your thoughts in the comments, and spreading the word with fellow travelers. You can also support my work by using my affiliate links for your bookings, engaging with my social media channels, or buying me a virtual coffee.
